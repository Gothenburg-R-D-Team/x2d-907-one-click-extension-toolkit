## 4. Preparation and limits of recovery

**Before upgrading, you must use the original computer and the original Software version to run “Restore original”. Confirm that recovery has completed before upgrading the desktop Software, then follow the new release's instructions to reinstall the camera extension.** “Original Software version” means the version of this toolkit used to install the extension currently on the camera, not the manufacturer's firmware version.

Although the Software supports recovery on another computer when using the same Software version, **always restore first with the original computer and original Software version before an upgrade, to avoid unnecessary problems caused by differences in versions, recovery materials or operating environments.** Support for recovery on another computer does not imply support for overwriting, installing or restoring across Software versions. Keep the original Software, complete package and relevant backups until recovery is complete; do not uninstall, overwrite or delete them first. If the original computer or version is unavailable, pause the upgrade, retain existing materials and ask through the project feedback channel for a confirmed recovery approach.

A first installation on a camera without this toolkit's extension does not require restoration of an existing installation.

Make sure you are authorised to operate the equipment. Follow the release instructions for compatible equipment, stable power, a reliable connection, necessary permissions and usable backups. Do not bypass model, firmware, integrity or other safety checks. Do not deliberately disconnect power or cables, shut equipment down during an operation, or run other tools that may interfere with camera communication.

**“Restore original”, “Uninstall” and similar actions are designed to withdraw changes managed by this tool as described for that release. They do not promise a return to any historical device state, repair of every firmware, hardware or storage fault, or recovery of photographs, warranty status or changes made by third-party software.** Missing backups, an unresponsive device, manufacturer updates, third-party modifications and other environmental changes may prevent recovery.

If a problem occurs, stop further experimentation and follow the release's troubleshooting instructions. Do not repeatedly attempt unverified installation, overwrite or recovery steps. Seek suitably qualified repair assistance when necessary.
